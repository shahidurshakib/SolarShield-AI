# SolarShield-AI exploratory data analysis

Generated by `python scripts/run_eda.py` from the prepared SILSO monthly data and cycle summary only.

## Scope and data

- Monthly observations: **3,333**, from **1749-01** through **2026-09**.
- Cycle-specific analyses exclude **73 pre-Cycle-1 months**. Cycles 1-24 are completed; Cycle 25 is incomplete and summarized only as observed to date.
- There are **3 provisional observation(s)** (2026-07, 2026-08, 2026-09). They are retained and marked in the applicable full-history and Cycle 25 plots and summaries; completed-cycle-only comparisons exclude them by design. Provisional values can be revised, so the current endpoint and Cycle 25 descriptive outputs are not definitive.
- Only the SILSO `ssn` series is analyzed. NOAA observations and forecasts are excluded; NOAA is not independent validation of SILSO. No model was trained and no forecasting was performed.

## Methods

- The full historical plot shows raw monthly SILSO values and published minimum-month markers. The individual plots cover each cycle, including ongoing Cycle 25. A triangle denotes a computed raw monthly peak; an open diamond denotes a **published 13-month-smoothed maximum**, not a raw monthly observation.
- Completed-cycle duration is the prepared published minimum-to-next-minimum interval in months. Raw peak and peak month are recomputed from monthly `ssn`. Months to peak count elapsed calendar months from the published minimum month (start is month 0). The published smoothed maximum and its month are kept in separate fields.
- Rise and decay descriptors are endpoint net changes per month: from the first monthly value to the raw monthly peak, and from that peak to the last observed month. These do not fit a smooth physical curve and should be interpreted cautiously because monthly values are noisy. Cycle 25 has no completed duration or decay-to-cycle-end measure.
- Normalized-phase CSV and plot include completed cycles only. For each cycle, the first observed month maps to phase 0 and the final observed month (the month before the next minimum) maps to phase 1; the monthly values are linearly interpolated to 101 equally spaced phase points. This removes calendar duration from the horizontal axis; phase is not calendar time.
- Distribution summaries report mean, median, sample standard deviation, coefficient of variation, quartiles, and observed range. Autocorrelation is calculated separately within each completed cycle for lags 1-24 months, so no pairs cross cycle boundaries. Correlations are descriptive Pearson and Spearman coefficients across completed cycles.
- Centered 13-month smoothing is not calculated or used. In particular, no centered smoother is used as a real-time or predictive feature.

## Findings

- The 24 completed cycles have durations from **108 to 163 months** (median 133.5); official published smoothed maxima range from **81.2 to 285.0**. Computed raw monthly peaks range from **103.8 to 398.2**. These ranges describe different quantities and must not be conflated.
- Time from cycle start to the raw monthly peak ranges from **33 to 83 months** (median 48.0). Endpoint net rise rates range from **1.31 to 10.79 SSN/month**; raw-peak-to-final-month net changes range from **-5.09 to -1.53 SSN/month**. These endpoint measures describe overall change, not fitted smooth rise/decay curves.
- The 3,178 monthly values assigned to completed cycles have mean **82.1**, median **67.5**, and sample standard deviation **68.2**. Per-cycle monthly sample standard deviations span **30.1 to 100.6**; month-level serial dependence means these are descriptive variability measures, not independent-sample uncertainty estimates.
- Across Cycle 1-24, the mean within-cycle lag-1 autocorrelation is **0.887** (cycle-level range 0.730 to 0.953). Autocorrelation indicates temporal dependence and is not evidence of predictive performance.
- Relationship coefficients are based on only 24 completed cycles:

| duration_months_minimum_to_minimum vs official_smoothed_max_sn_published | 24 | -0.324 | -0.314 |

## Limitations and unresolved issues

- The effective sample size for cycle-level relationships is at most **24 cycles**; cycle duration, peak strength, and peak timing are not independent across long historical records. Correlations are descriptive, sensitive to individual cycles, and do not establish causation.
- Monthly observations are serially dependent; monthly distributions and autocorrelation summaries should not be interpreted as independent samples. Cycle 25 is a partial cycle, so its distribution and raw peak are to-date statistics and are not comparable to completed-cycle durations or completed-cycle maxima.
- Provisional months (2026-07, 2026-08, 2026-09) are explicitly marked and counted. Any value revised later can affect endpoints, current-cycle descriptive statistics, and plots. The current Cycle 25 raw monthly peak is not provisional; that does not make the incomplete cycle final.
- The prepared cycle summary contains no official smoothed maximum for Cycle 25. This report does not substitute a computed or NOAA-derived value for a published maximum. See the preparation report for the unresolved source discrepancy concerning Cycle 25.
- Early-history `ssn_std` and `n_obs` availability does not affect this EDA because these fields are not used. The analysis is limited to the prepared inputs and does not independently validate their source lineage.
